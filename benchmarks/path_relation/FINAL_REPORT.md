# Road-relative path-entry experiment — final report\n\n**Decision: REJECT for demo integration.** The module is conservative about stale road masks and produced no false ENTERING/CROSSING events in this small replay, but exact state accuracy was only 39.13%, `IN_CORRIDOR` recall was 0%, half the reviewed entering/crossing events were missed, and temporal state switching was excessive. No live `--path-demo` flag or priority-map integration was added.\n\n## 1. Exact geometry approach\n\n`PathRelationEngine` consumes immutable tracked entities and the existing `RoadSegObservation`. All reasoning stays in image/mask space:\n\n1. Project the tracked box from its tracking source timestamp to the current update timestamp with the existing Kalman image-space velocity, bounded by tracking freshness.\n2. Use bottom-center as the object/road contact point.\n3. Map that point directly into the padded native 640×384 road-mask coordinates; the full mask is never resized for reasoning.\n4. Query cached summed-area tables over a small local rectangular foot patch.\n5. Compare current and 0.4-second predicted contact geometry with a cached conservative corridor.\n6. Apply per-track temporal persistence and expire disappeared tracks.\n\nThe result is the immutable `PathRelationObservation`, which preserves track ID, tracking source frame/time, road source frame/time, produced time, current/predicted contact, support fractions, state, confidence and reliability flags.\n\n## 2. Forward corridor construction\n\n- Work inside `RoadSegObservation.content_rect` at native mask resolution.\n- Search the lower-middle seed zone: x=42–58% and y=72–96% of content.\n- Choose the drivable seed nearest (50% width, 84% height), then flood-fill only its connected drivable component.\n- Reject components with less than 2.5% content area, inadequate lower-middle attachment, or fewer than 25% usable rows.\n- For each supported row, take the central 36% (±18% of that row’s component width) and intersect it with the connected road component.\n- The corridor can therefore never leave the selected drivable component. Poor/empty/disconnected masks produce `UNKNOWN`.\n\nThis is a **forward drivable corridor**, not an ego lane.\n\n## 3. Object road contact\n\n- `contact_x = (x1 + x2) / 2`; `contact_y = y2`.\n- Invalid/non-finite/reversed boxes abstain; valid contacts are clamped to image bounds.\n- Patch half-width is 18% of box width and half-height is 6% of box height after native-mask scaling, with a two-pixel minimum.\n- `drivable_fraction` and `corridor_overlap` are rectangular patch fractions from cached summed-area tables, not single pixels.\n- Road membership turns on at ≥0.60 and off at ≤0.25. Corridor membership turns on at ≥0.45 and off at ≤0.15. Values between thresholds retain prior membership.\n\n## 4. Path-relation states\n\n- `UNKNOWN`: unsupported class, stale/invalid tracking or road, untrusted corridor/contact, or insufficient geometry.\n- `OFF_DRIVABLE`: the contact patch has persistent weak road support.\n- `ON_DRIVABLE_OUTSIDE_CORRIDOR`: road-supported but outside the conservative corridor.\n- `ENTERING_CORRIDOR`: outside, moving meaningfully closer, and predicted into/near the corridor.\n- `IN_CORRIDOR`: current patch has strong corridor overlap without supported crossing/leaving evidence.\n- `CROSSING_CORRIDOR`: supported lateral movement traverses the corridor from a recent outside side.\n- `LEAVING_CORRIDOR`: currently/recently inside and consistently projected outward.\n\nOnly person, bicycle, motorcycle, car, truck and bus are supported.\n\n## 5. ENTERING decision\n\nENTERING requires all of the following:\n\n- current road membership is true;\n- current corridor membership is false;\n- motion is reliable;\n- current contact lies on a known outside side;\n- predicted contact remains on that side or reaches the corridor;\n- predicted distance is within 3.5% of image width;\n- distance improves by at least 1.2% of image width;\n- two consecutive unique tracking observations support the state.\n\nRepeated display frames from the same tracking source cannot satisfy persistence.\n\n## 6. CROSSING decision\n\nCROSSING requires reliable lateral motion of at least 2.5% image width/second plus either:\n\n- a recent outside side and a predicted opposite side, or\n- current corridor membership with a recent outside side and prediction through/to the opposite side.\n\nTwo consecutive unique tracking observations are required. This is image-space traversal only; it is not intent, collision probability or world motion.\n\n## 7. Temporal and hysteresis rules\n\n- Membership uses separate on/off thresholds listed above.\n- ENTERING, CROSSING and LEAVING require two consecutive unique tracking observations.\n- A history of eight samples is retained; motion reliability uses the most recent three.\n- Motion needs a 0.18–1.0 second three-sample span, a currently observed track, and bounded finite image velocity.\n- A repeated tracking source frame does not add evidence.\n- Missing tracks expire after 2.0 seconds.\n- Stationary, fresh tracks remain classifiable as on/off/in/out; motion-dependent states abstain unless history is reliable.\n\n## 8. Freshness rules\n\n- Tracking must be from a source frame no newer than the display frame and at most 0.75 seconds old.\n- Road geometry uses a stricter 0.55-second limit, versus the existing 1.35-second visualization limit. Replay showed false car transitions at 0.75-second road age, while the genuine pedestrian sequence used 0–0.5-second masks.\n- Track motion requires last observation age ≤0.45 seconds and no misses.\n- Old road timestamps are never refreshed. Result records retain both original tracking and road source metadata.\n- All 375 replay observations with road age >0.55 seconds returned `UNKNOWN` (100%).\n\n## 9. Confidence formula\n\nConfidence is a transparent support score, not a learned probability:\n\n`0.30*road_decisiveness + 0.25*corridor_decisiveness + 0.20*corridor_quality + 0.15*freshness + 0.06*temporal_support + 0.04*motion_support`\n\n- Road decisiveness is normalized distance from the midpoint of 0.25/0.60 thresholds.\n- Corridor decisiveness is normalized distance from the midpoint of 0.15/0.45 thresholds.\n- Freshness is the weaker normalized road/tracking freshness.\n- Temporal support saturates at four retained observations.\n- Motion support is 1.0 when reliable, otherwise 0.35.\n- `UNKNOWN` or missing support always has confidence 0.\n\n## 10. Acceptance-set size and distribution\n\nThe sealed set contains 69 human-reviewed track/frame items from the full 83.33-second `test_data/test_video.mp4` replay. Proposal states were retained separately and were not used as ground truth.\n\n| Human-reviewed state | Count |\n|---|---:|\n| ON_DRIVABLE_OUTSIDE_CORRIDOR | 31 |\n| IN_CORRIDOR | 16 |\n| OFF_DRIVABLE | 6 |\n| ENTERING_CORRIDOR | 6 |\n| LEAVING_CORRIDOR | 4 |\n| UNKNOWN | 4 |\n| CROSSING_CORRIDOR | 2 |\n\nClasses: 53 cars, 13 people, 2 trucks and 1 bus. Ground-truth SHA-256: `0cfba05862ee2ada164cbb06cfd8230ad538e6182690b7e15c943808d35d1d06`.\n\n## 11. Frame-level results\n\n- Exact state accuracy: **39.13% (27/69)**.\n- OFF_DRIVABLE: 83.33% recall, 13.51% precision.\n- ON_DRIVABLE_OUTSIDE_CORRIDOR: 54.84% recall, 68.00% precision.\n- IN_CORRIDOR: **0.00% recall**; 13/16 were called OFF_DRIVABLE.\n- ENTERING_CORRIDOR: 16.67% recall, 100% precision.\n- CROSSING_CORRIDOR: 50.00% recall, 100% precision.\n- LEAVING_CORRIDOR: 25.00% recall, 100% precision.\n- UNKNOWN: 50.00% recall and precision.\n\nThe dominant failure is segmentation support disappearing beneath vehicles, causing road-supported/in-corridor vehicles to be classified OFF_DRIVABLE.\n\n## 12. Event-level entering/crossing results\n\nTwo independently reviewed traversal events were available:\n\n| Event metric | ENTERING | CROSSING |\n|---|---:|---:|\n| Reviewed events | 2 | 2 |\n| Detected | 1 | 1 |\n| Missed | 1 | 1 |\n| False events | 0 | 0 |\n\n- Car #11 ENTERING: detected 1.0 s after first clear evidence; its CROSSING state was missed.\n- Person #17 ENTERING: missed; CROSSING detected with 0.0 s delay.\n- Both tracks retained the same ID throughout their reviewed state changes (2/2 continuity).\n\nZero false events on only two events is insufficient evidence of safety or generalization.\n\n## 13. State-flicker results\n\nAcross 1,523 unique tracking observations and 85 tracks:\n\n- 737 total state switches, including required transitions to/from stale-road `UNKNOWN`.\n- 81 switches after removing `UNKNOWN` states.\n- 85 one-observation non-UNKNOWN runs.\n\nThe 1 Hz road cadence plus 0.55-second spatial freshness creates predictable known/unknown alternation. More importantly, road support beneath objects also flickers between OFF and ON. Temporal stability is not acceptable for a user-facing state label.\n\n## 14. Compute performance\n\nFinal full offline replay, 2,000 display frames:\n\n| Metric | Time |\n|---|---:|\n| Mean | 0.831 ms |\n| Median | 0.494 ms |\n| P95 | 2.002 ms |\n| P99 | 6.352 ms |\n\nThe tail occurs when a new 1 Hz road mask triggers corridor construction. In the matched concurrent pipeline replay, path reasoning measured 1.222/0.732/2.837/9.379 ms mean/median/P95/P99. It is cheap relative to neural inference, but it does not meet the preferred “well below 1 ms” mean under full load.\n\n## 15. Red never means collision risk.\n\n## 17. Limitations\n\n- Only one 83-second urban video and two clear traversal events were available.\n- No reviewed bicycle or motorcycle case was present.\n- The road network often labels the visible road correctly but excludes pixels occupied by vehicles, undermining a centered foot patch.\n- A 1 Hz unwarped road mask cannot stay spatially aligned through significant ego motion for the existing 1.35-second overlay lifetime; path reasoning therefore abstains after 0.55 seconds.\n- Tracker velocity contains ego-camera motion and is not world-relative motion.\n- The central mask band is not a lane and can become semantically weak at intersections.\n- Exact state labels near corridor boundaries remain inherently image-space and camera-specific.\n- Confidence is support quality, not probability or calibration.\n\n## 18. ACCEPT / REJECT\n\n**REJECT.** Do not expose this module in the live demo or feed it into the Perception Priority Map.\n\nIt meets timestamp preservation and stale-road abstention, produces no false ENTERING/CROSSING events in this very small set, and has negligible pipeline throughput impact. It fails useful state accuracy, event recall, corridor occupancy recall, temporal stability and preferred compute-tail targets.\n\nNo collision, TTC, metric distance, world velocity, intent, steering, planning or control claim was implemented.\n\n## 19. Optional demo command\n\n**Not applicable because the module was rejected.** No `--path-demo` flag was added. The existing demo and risk demo remain unchanged.\n\n## 20. Exact next technical step\n\nBuild a geometry-only v2 (still offline) that addresses the measured failure rather than changing thresholds:\n\n1. Replace the symmetric foot patch with a road-support probe immediately below the box plus small left/right flank probes, so an object does not erase the road evidence beneath itself.\n2. Warp reused road masks with the existing bounded `MotionSnapshot` affine chain before path sampling; return UNKNOWN when that chain is incomplete.\n3. Separate corridor refresh cost from per-track updates and cache a typed corridor observation with the original road source metadata.\n4. Use the current 69 items as development diagnostics, then create a new reviewed acceptance slice from unused timestamps/videos.\n5. Require materially higher IN/ON recall, both entering/crossing events detected, no new false transition events, and reduced non-UNKNOWN flicker before reconsidering demo integration.\n\nDo not add another neural network and do not modify the paused ego-lane artifacts.\n
